Big picture UI sounds
=====================

Sound effects from CONSOLE_MODE_UI_SOUNDS_KAGE by Brian Kage (briankage.com).

Used with the author's terms: free for non-commercial use. Anyone who wants to use
these sounds in a commercial project needs the author's permission first
(michiganderrecords@gmail.com).

Files, and the original sample each one came from:
  move.wav    NAV_1
  key.wav     NAV_5
  enter.wav   A CONFIRM_6
  select.wav  A CONFIRM_9
  back.wav    BACK_4
  tab.wav     Z BONUS NAV
  launch.wav  A CONFIRM_7
  pause.wav   A CONFIRM_3
  resume.wav  Z BONUS BACK
  quit.wav    BACK_7

Every sample in the pack is kept, under their original names, in OpenEmu/BigPictureSoundPack/ (not bundled)
so other sounds can be tried by copying one over the matching file here.
