## Controller illustration history

Derek Hugger created the controller illustration in Adobe Illustrator. The original version used an online product photograph of a New Nintendo 3DS XL as a visual reference; some geometry was traced, and the shading, screens, reflections, and other graphical treatments were created by the artist. The identity and re-use license of that third-party photograph have not been established.

On November 8, 2026, Derek photographed his own 3DS console from similar viewpoints and revised the controller illustration using those photographs. The revisions adjusted the positions of the Home button, D-pad, Circle Pad, face buttons, and C-stick. The console silhouette and artist-created shading were substantially retained. The third-party photograph is **not** distributed with the repository.

The artist reported that the revised illustration successfully built and displayed in OpenEmu, with the controller mask operating as expected. The obsolete NDS reference images were removed from the local test project without observed problems and were already absent from the private GitHub source snapshot.

This record describes creation and testing history; it does **not** claim the illustration was originally derived exclusively from the artist's photographs. The revision reduces reliance on an unverified external reference but does not by itself establish legal clearance for every pre-existing traced element. Keep this distinction in any subsequent public release review.
