CONSOLE_MODE_UI_SOUNDS_KAGE
===========================

Thank you for downloading this sound pack! I created these samples to contribute to the retro gaming community and enhance the user experience for the Superstation One Console Mode UI.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
--------------
* 33 Audio Samples designed specifically for console-style UI navigation.

HOW TO USE
----------
1. Using your SD card, place the sound effects into the Sounds / SystemSounds directory.
2. Open Console Mode on your device and navigate to Sound & Connectivity / System Sounds.
3. Choose your desired sounds from the menu.

Feel free to mix and match and experiment to get the setup you like best!
